Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Flip & Guess: Word Puzzle (the “Application”), published on Google Play by Dhirajlal Zalavadiya (the “developer”, “we”, “us”), handles information.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application does not require an account, sign-in, or registration, and the Developer does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or any other information that directly identifies you.

Player nicknames you type in, your game settings, selected categories and game progress are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to the Developer.

The Application uses third-party services that may collect limited technical information, including the device Internet Protocol (IP) address, device model and operating system version, advertising identifiers, application version, and anonymous usage and diagnostic events. This information is used to keep the Application working, understand which features are used, diagnose crashes, and deliver advertising. See section 4.

The Application does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files, and does not send push notifications.

2. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

The Application offers an optional paid upgrade (“Pro”), available as an auto-renewing subscription and/or a one-time purchase. Buying Pro is entirely optional; the Application is fully playable without it.

  • All payments are processed by the official application store through which the Application was downloaded. The Developer never receives, sees or stores your card number, bank details or billing address.
  • Purchases and subscription status are managed through RevenueCat, Inc., a third-party subscription-management service. To validate a purchase and restore it across your devices, RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous application-generated identifier together with purchase and receipt data such as the product purchased, purchase and expiry dates, store, country and platform. This data is not used to identify you personally and is not sold.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled before the end of the current billing period. Subscriptions can be viewed, managed or cancelled at any time through the account settings of the application store used for the purchase, and not within the Application.
  • Where a free trial is offered, any unused portion of that trial is forfeited when a subscription is purchased.

RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

3. Advertising

For users who have not purchased Pro, the Application displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob. Purchasing Pro removes all advertising from the Application.

  • AdMob and its advertising partners may use device and advertising identifiers to select and measure advertisements and to limit how often the same advertisement is shown.
  • Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent, through Google’s User Messaging Platform, before personalised advertising is used. This choice can be reviewed and changed at any time from within the Application.
  • Advertising identifiers can also be reset or limited using the privacy controls provided by your device’s operating system.

Information on how Google uses data from applications that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Third-Party Services

The Application uses the following third-party services. Each processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous usage and event analytics. Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and stability diagnostics. Policy
  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform — advertising and advertising consent. Policy
  • RevenueCat, Inc. — purchase validation and subscription management. Policy

The Developer does not sell your personal information, and does not share your data with any party other than the service providers listed above for the purposes described in this policy.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Game data, nicknames and settings remain on your device until you clear the Application’s stored data or uninstall the Application, which removes all information held locally.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any data held about you by the Developer’s service providers, and may withdraw consent for personalised advertising at any time. Such requests can be made using the contact details in section 9 and are actioned within 30 days.

Records of a purchase or subscription may be retained by the relevant application store or by RevenueCat for as long as required for billing, tax and legal purposes, even after the Application has been uninstalled.

6. Children

The Application is intended for a general audience aged 16 and above and is not directed at children. The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please use the contact details in section 9 and it will be deleted.

7. Security

Because information created in the Application is stored locally, its security depends on the protections available on your device, such as a screen lock and device encryption. Data handled by the third-party services listed above is protected under their own security practices. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.
