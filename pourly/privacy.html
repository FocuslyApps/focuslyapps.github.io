Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Pourly: Water Sort Puzzle (the “Application”), published on Google Play by Dhirajlal Zalavadiya (the “developer”, “we”, “us”), handles information.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application does not require an account, sign-in, or registration, and the Developer does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or any other information that directly identifies you.

Your game progress, stars, coins, daily streak, unlocked bottles, scenes and colors, achievements and settings are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to the Developer.

The Application uses third-party services that may collect limited technical information, including the device Internet Protocol (IP) address, device model and operating system version, advertising identifiers, application version, and anonymous usage and diagnostic events. This information is used to keep the Application working, understand which features are used, diagnose crashes, and deliver advertising. See section 5.

The Application does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files.

2. Notifications

With your permission, the Application shows an optional daily reminder (for example, that a new Daily Puzzle or daily gift is ready). These reminders are created and scheduled entirely on your device — no push-notification service is used and no data is transmitted for this purpose. You can change the reminder time or turn reminders off at any time in the Application’s settings or in your device settings.

3. Moving Progress to a New Phone

The Application can create a transfer code that contains your saved game progress, so you can copy it to a new phone yourself. The code is created on your device and is not sent to the Developer or to any server. It contains no personal information and no purchase data. Anyone who has your code can load your progress, so keep it to yourself.

4. In-App Purchases

The Application offers optional one-time purchases: Remove Ads, Pourly Plus and packs of in-game coins. Nothing is a subscription and nothing renews. Buying is entirely optional; the Application is fully playable without any purchase.

  • All payments are processed by the official application store through which the Application was downloaded. The Developer never receives, sees or stores your card number, bank details or billing address.
  • Purchases are managed through RevenueCat, Inc., a third-party purchase-management service. To validate a purchase and restore it across your devices, RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous application-generated identifier together with purchase and receipt data such as the product purchased, purchase date, store, country and platform. This data is not used to identify you personally and is not sold.
  • Remove Ads and Pourly Plus can be restored on another device with “Restore Purchases”. In-game coins are saved on your device only; they can be moved to a new phone with the transfer code described in section 3.

RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

5. Advertising

The Application displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob, including banner ads, full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. Purchasing Remove Ads or Pourly Plus removes banner and full-screen ads; optional rewarded videos remain available only if you choose to watch them.

  • AdMob and its advertising partners may use device and advertising identifiers to select and measure advertisements and to limit how often the same advertisement is shown.
  • Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent, through Google’s User Messaging Platform, before personalised advertising is used. This choice can be reviewed and changed at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices within the Application.
  • On iOS, the Application asks for permission before using the device advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the advertising identifier is not used.
  • Advertising identifiers can also be reset or limited using the privacy controls provided by your device’s operating system.

Information on how Google uses data from applications that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Third-Party Services

The Application uses the following third-party services. Each processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous usage and event analytics. Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and stability diagnostics. Policy
  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform — advertising and advertising consent. Policy
  • RevenueCat, Inc. — purchase validation and restoring purchases. Policy

The Developer does not sell your personal information, and does not share your data with any party other than the service providers listed above for the purposes described in this policy.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress, coins and settings remain on your device until you clear the Application’s stored data or uninstall the Application, which removes all information held locally.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any data held about you by the Developer’s service providers, and may withdraw consent for personalised advertising at any time. Such requests can be made using the contact details in section 11 and are actioned within 30 days.

Records of a purchase may be retained by the relevant application store or by RevenueCat for as long as required for billing, tax and legal purposes, even after the Application has been uninstalled.

8. Children

The Application is intended for a general audience aged 16 and above and is not directed at children. The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please use the contact details in section 11 and it will be deleted.

9. Security

Because information created in the Application is stored locally, its security depends on the protections available on your device, such as a screen lock and device encryption. Data handled by the third-party services listed above is protected under their own security practices. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Continued use of the Application after a change indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
